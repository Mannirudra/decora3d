RETURN & REFUND POLICY

7-DAY RETURN WINDOW

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for items that arrive damaged, defective, or significantly different from the product listing. We do not accept returns for change of mind.

NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS

  • Custom / personalised products (name keychains, custom nameplates, bespoke designs)
  • Products that have been used, modified, or assembled
  • Workshop bookings (see cancellation policy below)
  • Corporate bulk orders once production has commenced

HOW TO INITIATE A RETURN

  1. WhatsApp us at +91 XXXXXXXXXX within 7 days of delivery
  2. Send clear photos of the damage / defect
  3. Our team will review and approve within 1 business day
  4. We'll arrange a pickup from your address at no cost
  5. Refund processed within 5–7 business days after we receive the item

WORKSHOP CANCELLATION

48+ hours before session: Full refund or free reschedule.

Less than 48 hours: No refund. Reschedule possible (one-time only).

REFUND TIMELINE

Refunds are processed to the original payment method (UPI, card, bank account) within 5–7 business days after the return is approved. Razorpay refunds may take an additional 2–3 days depending on your bank.
