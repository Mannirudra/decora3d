Decora3D is Delhi NCR's first 3D printing experience studio — a place where technology meets creativity, and your ideas become tangible, beautiful objects.

WHO WE ARE

We're a team of designers, engineers, and makers obsessed with one question: what if your home could look like the future?

Founded in Delhi NCR, Decora3D began with a single printer and a conviction that 3D printing deserved a place in every Indian home — not as a technology novelty, but as a genuine craft. Today, we operate from a 1040 sq.ft studio with multiple FDM and SLA machines, producing unique, made-to-order home decor, corporate gifts, and educational workshops.

THE TECHNOLOGY

FDM PRINTING

Fused Deposition Modelling — plastic filament (PLA+, PETG) is melted and extruded layer by layer. Strong, colourful, and sustainable. Used for 90% of our products.

SLA RESIN PRINTING

Stereolithography — liquid resin is cured with UV light, layer by microscopic layer. Ultra-fine detail, smooth surfaces. Used for our Ganesha and premium decor pieces.

Our primary filament is PLA+ — derived from sugarcane and corn starch. It's biodegradable, non-toxic, and produces stunning results. We source from certified manufacturers who maintain consistent quality for colour accuracy across every batch.
